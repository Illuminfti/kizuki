Codex

The Mind Codex depth layer. The section hero and overview render from the local /api/codex and /api/actions responses, marked Live. Card variants and edge states use inputs marked Sample.

Section hero

Compact banner with the section's own art and tone and four counts that no plate below repeats (entries, pinned, entries without a next move, one section-specific figure). Below it, three plates: where entries live (tap a bar to filter), conviction or the idea pipeline, and people in orbit with initials avatars. A section with fewer than three linked people shows entries the matcher flagged as possible taste conflicts, labelled as unconfirmed. Under 640px the plates become a swipeable strip.

Codex card

Flat plate, no generated cover. Domain is muted text with a section-tone dot, pinned entries get a small star mark. Conviction is a dot and a word. Thesis labels are sentence case. Linked people get initials avatars. Board, doc, pin and edit become icon buttons with labels for assistive tech. Summaries over 260 characters clamp to four lines with a separate Show more button. A card without a leverage score hides the meter instead of showing a fallback number.
