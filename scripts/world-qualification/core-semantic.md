# Core semantic qualification contract

The current prospective split uses `core-semantic-v2` at `prepared_admission` for Concept and Situation extraction. Read [the production preparation method](core-semantic-v2.md) and the exact frozen [case contract](core-semantic-contract.json) together. Neither method code nor a scripted control qualifies a model.

The twelve fresh synthetic records cover a direct positive, a person's report, a historical document report, a bare name, a blank template, and a hypothetical for each kind. The source and expected meanings were authored and independently reviewed before any call. The prompt and production-normalizer authors did not see these records. The corpus author inspected preceding schema/fixture prefixes; this is fresh prospective evidence, without a claim of strict-blind authorship.

Required meanings, actor roles, explicit exclusive validity intervals, negative abstention, and forbidden ownership/identity/currentness claims are fixed before the run. Direct headers establish the two person-report names independently of their reported details. A document is a holder rather than a person speaker. Historical labels remain document-attributed and retain their own interval. Qualified labels are authorized search aliases and visible relations, never bare display labels.

The raw transport and parsed responses remain unchanged. Raw citation precision is reported separately. Actual public preparation must preserve the original guard, meanings, body, references, attribution, confidence, authority, time and original anchors, with zero drops and one admission per claim. Its only extra evidence is the exact already-sent window appended after the original guard. Independent review must establish the particular role-to-fact relationship; unrelated words elsewhere in a window cannot establish it.

Acceptance requires all automated checks and an independent review bound to the exact report, source commit, corpus, contract, raw transport and parsed responses, input/window manifests and prepared admissions. Previous campaigns remain rejected and are never rescored under this method. The new run remains pending until its own actual outputs and endpoint evidence pass the real verifier.
