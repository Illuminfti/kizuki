# Prospective world union qualification

This fixed `core-semantic-v4` / `union22` method is prospective. The canonical
nine-kind registry is active, but this source method supplies no model or native
consumer evidence. A fresh complete22-case run and separately pinned independent
semantic/body review are required before any qualification decision.

## Fixed source and meaning

The corpus preserves the existing17 source case objects and contract rows exactly.
The five appended groups contain90 propositions: direct Question/Person20,
attributed/historical Question/Person12, a combined four-record abstention,
a coherent Skill/Framework/Procedure guide31, and a typed Commitment/Decision
and Situation episode27. This covers the70 offered world predicate rows under
one canonical nine-kind profile and its six Situation extension rows. Predicate
coverage is not coverage of every enum, alternative object form or lifecycle.

The active product and qualification runner both compose from
`createWorldUnionCandidateRegistry` and its frozen kind/extension constants in
Core. Constructing the profile does not attest qualification; release gating
still requires a separate accepted receipt and independent review.

The accepted Question/Person draft content and the final union source/oracle
received independent prospective review. Those content reviews are neither
method approval nor actual output reviews. No old13/17 campaign receipt is
composed, relabeled or rescored as union qualification.

A complete branch is selected as a whole. Scoped proposition candidates are
preselected by exact predicate, resolved subject and subject-object direction,
object kind/vocabulary value, polarity, complete supported perspective variant,
valid interval and context, then exactly one candidate is required. Alternative
perspectives are complete authored structures; fields never mix. A claim cannot
fill two rules. Duplicate, omitted, unsupported and uncontracted typed claims
refuse. A contracted typed predicate remains typed even if its ID also occurs
in the legacy registry (`commitment.due`); no wrong-subject due claim may hide
among legacy extras. The frozen v3 general matcher retains its existing path.

The inherited cutting-relations case has exactly two scoped v4 alternatives.
`complete-local-alias` retains p1 through p11. `partial-local-alias` omits only
p6, the duplicate Ochre definition. Both bind an explicit positive, asserted,
explicit Concept-to-Concept `same_as` relation and may nominate no candidate or
exactly the earlier Thistle occurrence in the same source record. The nomination
is local response metadata only: supplied, mixed, stale, multiple, later,
cross-record, or chained candidates refuse and never create a durable merge or
host grounding.

Source occurrence selectors bind one exact allowed mention occurrence, never
an arbitrary equal spelling. Structured selectors use actual
`worldSuppliedReferences` and the source record namespace. Equal native subject
IDs/display names in different records remain distinct. Trial/sample/document
handles remain unclassified raw endpoints unless the source independently
supports a typed classification. A grounded document holder is never a person
speaker merely because it has a supplied handle.

The explicit `selected_entity_label/v1` exception applies only to the selected
kind's registered literal label predicate (including `question.text`). It
requires the exact bound source name/text and that SAME selected mention or
genuine supplied name anchor. It waives no classification, substantive,
attribution or temporal witness. Every other domain witness remains complete;
independent role/time spans remain mandatory on that claim. Temporal equality
uses exact RFC3339 instants as in v3, including an exclusive end; capture/known-at
clocks confer no validity. The source's Procedure validation date is an authored
RFC3339 instant; no midnight precision is invented from a calendar date.

Literal objects are copied from source and reviewed with natural closed atoms,
not a finite literal whitelist or model grader. The actual complete SPO must
supply its meaning; body, neighboring claims and large evidence windows cannot
repair omitted content. Body fidelity, forbidden interpretations, source
entailment and legacy extras require independent actual-claim review. Raw and
prepared semantics must both pass; a source interest asserted as certain does
not qualify because production later downgrades it to uncertain/inferred.

## Custody and limits

Existing production planner, wire decoder, closed parser, guards and preparation
remain the only mechanical path. V4 preserves the exact input/sent-window,
raw model text, adapter resolution, actual per-rule claim, branch and prepared
admission hashes. It reuses the v3 transport observation and v1 wire/receipt
formats; the v4 report/result/review schemas distinguish the new semantics.
No output normalizer, salvage, synthetic answer substitution or response-file
runner is provided.

The fixed input policy is32000 total input tokens,24000 trusted configured
input-overhead allowance and8192 output tokens. The allowance is conservative
configuration, not a measured guarantee. Production planning must fit every
actual input, and measured input/output usage remains authoritative after each
call. Transport retries remain0. Missing or different v4 policy refuses before
transport. V3 defaults and policies remain unchanged.

Limits remain32 propositions,8 authored source-local occurrence entities,
8 source records per request,8 structured subjects per record,4 contexts per
proposition,3 complete branches and4 complete perspective variants. No bound
was widened to pack this split. Exact production wire descriptor resolution is
mandatory even for repeated source names. Scripted reference-output sizing only
shows that a legal example fits; it is not measured provider usage or a promise
that actual model output will fit. Larger ordinary source records may need
supported production segmentation; this corpus proves no larger-window bound.

A run has one fixed ordered22-case selection, no subset, resume or retry. It
halts after the first transport, schema, budget, semantic-structure or custody
failure. All attempts and honest usage remain recorded. A partial or failed run
cannot qualify. The runner always exits1 pending independent review even after
all automated cases pass; inspect `halt_reason`, attempts and full case states.

## Commands after separate authorization

The normal entrypoint explicitly opts into the new protocol and fixed policy:

```bash
bun --no-env-file --config=/dev/null scripts/world-qualification/run.ts \
  --protocol core-semantic-v4 --core-split union22 \
  --kinds concept,situation,question,person,skill,framework,procedure,commitment,decision \
  --extensions situation.has_commitment \
  --max-input-tokens 32000 --input-token-overhead 24000 --max-output-tokens 8192 \
  --out /absolute/new/report.json --raw-responses /absolute/new/raw-responses.jsonl
```

Without explicitly authorized endpoint/model/credential arguments this produces
no model calls. Do not use this example as runtime or spending authorization.
The existing canonical verifier takes the full report, its exact hash, a
separately authored independent review under the fixture directory, its hash,
and the independent endpoint evidence hash:

```bash
bun --no-env-file --config=/dev/null scripts/world-qualification/verify-core-semantic.ts \
  --core-split union22 --report /absolute/frozen/report.json \
  --report-sha256 REPORT_SHA --review-file union-review.json \
  --review-sha256 REVIEW_SHA --endpoint-evidence ENDPOINT_SHA
```

Neither a generated review template nor an arbitrary endpoint digest proves
semantic correctness or provider identity. The operator must retain actual raw
archive reconciliation, quota/meter/bridge controls, independent evidence and
native consumer gates. V4 registry verification requires the canonical profile,
exact complete22 trace/observation sequence, frozen source/runner/corpus/contract,
measured usage, full custody and independent review. There is no approval waiver.

## Later consumer acceptance

Read actual extracted evidence, not only default fields or nonempty arrays.
Question reported partial status remains `statusEvidence`; its lifecycle stays
open without native-owner status or qualified independent answers, and
understanding remains unknown. Person interest is an uncertain/inferred relation
inside perspectives; its dated relationship needs explicit valid-time windows.
An always-null `agreedDeadline` alone proves no extraction.

Skill evidence rows do not prove mastery or usefulness; the linked raw inspection
is not an independently resolved outcome observation. Framework arrays preserve
qualifications and do not imply adoption. Procedure steps are quoted assertions,
execution authority remains none, and a recorded validation timestamp is not
successful/current execution proof. Negative/reported/hypothetical rows in arrays
retain those modes rather than becoming affirmative facts.

Read Situation v2 explicitly for typed Commitment/Decision nodes. Asserted positive
links need eligible classified endpoints and genuine supplied Person parties.
An open promise/decision is not done, agreed, chosen or performed. A calendar due
date is not an invented midnight deadline. Unknown-valid claims are not present
proof under an at/overlap filter. Native CLI/MCP/HTTP evidence, authorization,
withdrawal, correction/undo and cross-client behavior remain separate acceptance
checks; none is claimed by scripted evaluator tests.
